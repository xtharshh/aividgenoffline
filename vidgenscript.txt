
Welcome to the offline AI avatar studio.
In this short demo, I will show the full flow on the local web page.

First, open the Avatar Generator Studio page in the browser.
Now upload an avatar image of your presenter.
Next, upload this script file, or paste the script into the script text area.

For speech, choose the TTS engine.
If you select Piper, set Piper voice to Male, Ryan.
If you want a custom male voice, choose XTTS and upload a male voice sample.

Now upload a screen recording so it appears behind the avatar as picture in picture.
Set the PiP layout to bottom right for a classic tutorial look.
Choose Fast mode for quick tests, or Balanced for better quality.

Click Generate video and wait for the live logs.
The system runs text to speech, avatar animation, subtitles, and final composition.
When the job finishes, download the MP4 result.

That is the complete local workflow.
You now have a synced avatar and screen demo video generated fully offline.
